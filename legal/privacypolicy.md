This policy applies to InGroups. Our general company privacy practices for other products may be described separately at **https://annaliese-sebastian.com**.

If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the Service.

## 1. Information We Collect

We practice data minimization. Depending on how you use the Service, we may collect the following:

### Account and identity information

- **Email address, name, and authentication identifiers** when you sign in with email, Google, or similar methods through our authentication provider (**Clerk**).
- **Work or school identity information** (such as display name, email address, and platform user identifiers) when you use InGroups inside a **meeting platform integration** and sign in through that platform’s single sign-on (SSO) or authorization flow (for example, Microsoft Entra ID in Teams, or equivalent flows on other platforms).
- **Clerk user ID** and related account metadata stored in our database to associate your account with licenses and saved content.

### Guest play

- If you play without signing in, we may assign a **guest identifier** and use a **display name** you provide. Guest sessions are not linked to a persistent account unless you later sign in.

### Gameplay and session information

- **Display names**, **room codes**, **in-game chat messages**, scores, guesses, and related gameplay state while you participate in a game session.
- Game rooms and most gameplay data are held **in server memory** for the duration of the session and are **not** stored long-term as a gameplay history after the room ends, except as needed for real-time operation of the Service.

### User-created content

- **Custom word sets** (names and word lists) that you create and save to your account are stored in our database.

### License and purchase information

- **License keys**, activation status, and purchase records associated with your account.
- **Stripe checkout session and payment metadata** (such as amount, quantity, and transaction status). We do **not** receive or store your full payment card or bank account numbers; **Stripe** processes payments.

### Technical and usage information

- **IP address**, browser or client type, device information, and request logs collected automatically by our hosting and infrastructure providers for security, debugging, and operation of the Service.
- **Error and performance data** that may be logged by our servers or providers when something fails.

### Support communications

- Information you send us by email or other support channels (such as your email address and message content).

## 4. Data Retention

- **Account, license, and custom word set data** is retained while your account exists and as needed to provide the Service and meet legal obligations.
- **Gameplay session data** (rooms, chat, scores during an active game) is generally retained only for the duration of the session on our game servers.
- **Server and security logs** may be retained for a limited period for troubleshooting and security.
- **Purchase records** may be retained as required for accounting, tax, and legal compliance.

When we no longer need information, we take reasonable steps to delete or de-identify it.

## 5. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict certain processing of your personal information.

- **Account data:** You may update some information through your account profile (via Clerk) where available.
- **Deletion requests:** Email **contact@annaliese-sebastian.com** with the subject line **"Data Deletion Request"** and enough information for us to identify your account. We will respond as required by applicable law.
