## About InGroups

**InGroups** is a multiplayer word game for teams and meetings. Players are split into groups each round: one **In Group** discusses and tries to agree on one word from a grid, while **Out Groups** listen and try to guess that word. It runs in a web browser and inside **Microsoft Teams** (personal app, channel tab, and meeting side panel). Hosts and players in Teams and on the web can join the **same lobby** using a shared room code.

## Using InGroups in Microsoft Teams

**Personal app**

1. Open **Microsoft Teams** (desktop or web).
2. Go to **Apps**, search for **InGroups**, and open or pin the app.

**Channel tab**

1. Open the team and channel where you want the tab.
2. Select **+** (Add a tab), choose **InGroups**, complete configuration if prompted, and save.

**Meeting side panel**

1. Join or start a meeting in Teams.
2. Open **Apps**, select **InGroups**, and open the side panel.

**Cross-client play:** A host can create a room in Teams while other players join from the web app (or the reverse) using the same **room code**.

## How to Play

### Setup

Players are divided into groups. Each round, one group is the **In Group**. Click one of the 20 word buttons to lock in your guess.

### In Group

Talk out loud and try to agree on one word from the grid. Use shared context carefully so Out Groups cannot infer your choice too easily.

### Out Groups

Pay attention to the In Group and guess which word the In Group will choose.

When a **round timer** is enabled, the In Group has until the timer expires to select a word; Out Groups then have time to submit guesses. When the timer is off, the round ends when all players have selected a word.

### Scoring

Scoring rules are evaluated **in order**; the first matching rule applies. If none match, no points are awarded for that check.

- Every member of an Out Group guesses the In Group's most popular word → **+2** to each member of that Out Group
- At least as many members of an Out Group guess the In Group's most popular word → **+1** to each member of that Out Group
- All In Group members choose the same word → **+2** to each In Group member
- Two or more In Group members agree on a word, but not all → **+1** to each In Group member

If the host enables an **In Group Speed Bonus** on timed rounds, the In Group may earn extra points when they score on the round **and** lock in quickly:

- 75% or more of timer remaining → **+3** bonus
- 50% or more → **+2** bonus
- 25% or more → **+1** bonus

## Host settings

Click **Host New Game** (or **Host Game**) to open a lobby and share the **room code** with other players.

On the lobby screen, the host can set the **score to win** (or disable the win condition for open-ended play). The host controls teams, timer, scoring options, and word set between rounds. There must be at least **2** players in the In Group and at least **1** player in each Out Group to start.

- **Shuffle Group:** Randomly assigns players to groups.
- **Switch In Group:** Rotates which group is the In Group while keeping assignments.
- **Player menus (In Group / Out Group / Scoreboard):** Move a player to another group or remove them from the game.
- **Reset Scores:** Clears scores from the Scoreboard menu.
- **Select Word Sets:** Choose the word set for the next round.
- **Round Timer:** Set In Group time limit, or turn the timer off.
- **In Group Speed Bonus:** Enable or disable speed bonus when the timer is on.
- **Start Round / End Round:** Begin the round or end it early.

## Premium options

When signed in (not as Guest), open the **License** section to purchase or activate a license. A license unlocks:

- **50+ premium word sets**
- **Custom word sets** — from the main menu, choose **Create Word Set**, name the set, enter at least 20 words (one per line), and save. Edit or delete your sets from the same area.

One license applies to one account. You may share unused license keys with others; they enter the key on the **License** page.
